# Suiteframe mood-image prompts

Paste the **House bible** at the start of every prompt, word for word, so every room is the same house. Add the **room line**, then the **Photo style** suffix.

## House bible

A converted 18th-century farmhouse at 1,600 m on a steep meadow in a high Alpine valley in Graubünden, Switzerland. Exterior: thick walls of rough, hand-laid grey granite fieldstone with irregular lime mortar; upper gable clad in vertical larch boards weathered to silver-brown; steep roof of grey stone slabs with a thin dark metal edge. Few, deep windows: small square original openings with lime-washed reveals, and two large new frameless openings cut into the gable, lined in thin warm oak. A cantilevered larch terrace with vertical slatted balustrade on a smooth board-formed concrete base. Late-autumn grass, larch forest turning gold, snow-dusted peaks behind.

Interior: the old stone shell stays raw on the inside of the outer walls; inside it sits a new, precise insertion. Warm greige micro-cement floors, white-oiled oak joinery with flush handle-less doors, pale sanded original roof trusses over a double-height living space, deep oak-lined window seats set into the thickness of the stone walls. Strict material palette: grey granite, pale oak, warm off-white lime plaster, honed dark quartzite in the bathrooms, brushed bronze fittings, wool and linen in oatmeal, charcoal and one muted moss green. Furniture is low, few and architectural: a long linen sofa, a solid oak table, a handful of classic Swiss and Scandinavian pieces, no decorative clutter. Light: warm 2700K concealed linear light and one sculptural pendant indoors; soft, cool valley daylight outside. Mood: quiet, monastic luxury, contemporary Graubünden architecture.

## Photo style suffix

Architectural interiors photography, medium format, 35mm tilt-shift, eye level, perfectly straight verticals, natural light, muted colours, soft contrast, editorial magazine look. No people, no text, no logos.
